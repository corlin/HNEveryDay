隐私政策

生效日期： 2026年10月25日

欢迎使用“HNEveryDay”（以下简称“本应用”）。不仅我们致力于为您提供优质的服务，更非常重视您的隐私保护。本《隐私政策》将向您说明我们在您使用服务时如何收集、使用、存储和保护您的个人信息，以及您享有的权利。

请您在使用本应用前仔细阅读并确认您已经充分理解本隐私政策所写明的内容。

1. 我们如何收集和使用您的信息

本应用主要通过 HackerNews API 获取公开资讯，并在您的设备上进行展示。我们仅遵循正当、合法、必要的原则收集最少量的必要信息：

  • 网络通讯： 本应用需要访问网络权限以连接 HackerNews 服务器获取最新的信息列表、评论和用户详情。网络请求日志（如IP地址）可能会被 API 提供方（Firebase
  • 本地存储： 本应用可能会在您的设备本地存储您的浏览历史、收藏（书签）等数据，以便为您提供更好的用户体验。这些数据仅存储在您的设备及其关联的 iCloud 账户中，我们不会将其上传至其他服务器。
  • 网页浏览： 当您点击信息链接时，本应用会通过内置浏览器（WebView）加载第三方网页。第三方网站可能会使用 Cookies 或收集您的访问信息，请您参考该第三方网站的隐私政策。
  • AI 功能（托管模式，默认）： 使用标题翻译、摘要、全文翻译或 Agent 时，相关文章标识、您设备上提取的正文摘录以及您输入的问题会发送到 HNEveryDay 服务器（运行于 Cloudflare），再由服务器转发给模型服务商（DeepSeek）生成结果。标题翻译与摘要会作为公共内容缓存在服务器上；全文译文和 Agent 对话不会在服务器上保存。
  • 匿名设备标识： 为防止滥用并计算每日额度，应用会通过 Apple App Attest 生成一个与您身份无关的匿名设备标识。我们不收集姓名、邮箱、Apple ID 或广告标识符。
  • 订阅： 购买 Pro 时，Apple 提供的签名交易信息（交易编号、产品、到期时间）会发送到我们的服务器用于确认权益。我们无法获得您的支付信息。
  • 自带 API Key（高级模式）： 选择此模式后，摘要、翻译与 Agent 对话直接从您的设备发送到您配置的服务商，不经过 HNEveryDay 服务器；API Key 仅保存在设备钥匙串中。唯一例外是 Agent 的推荐问题：应用只向 HNEveryDay 服务器发送公开的文章编号与语言，获取所有用户共享的推荐问题。

2. 信息的存储

存储地点： 本应用产生的数据（如收藏、设置）主要存储在您的本地设备上。

存储期限： 除非您主动删除或卸载应用，否则本地数据将一直保留。

3. 信息共享、转让和公开披露

我们不会出售或公开披露您的个人信息。本应用没有账号注册系统，我们不持有您的个人身份信息。为提供 AI 功能，第 1 节所述数据会交由以下处理方：Cloudflare（服务器托管）、DeepSeek（模型推理）、Apple（购买与设备校验）。服务器日志与使用计数最多保留 90 天。

Cloudflare 的服务器可能位于中国境外，DeepSeek 的模型服务位于中国境内。发送的内容仅限上述文章摘录与提问，不包含可识别您身份的信息。AI 生成的内容在应用内标注“AI 生成”，复制或分享时同样保留该标识；您可以通过“举报”按钮反馈不当内容，我们会核实并下线。

4. 权限说明

本应用申请的权限仅用于提供核心功能：

  • 网络权限 (Internet)： 用于获取新闻数据和加载网页。

本应用不涉及相机、相册、地理位置、日历、麦克风等敏感权限的申请和使用。

5. 未成年人保护

我们非常重视对未成年人个人信息的保护。如果您是未满18周岁的未成年人，在使用本应用前，应在您的父母或其他监护人的监护、指导下共同阅读并同意本隐私政策。

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

Welcome to use "HNEveryDay" (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). We are committed to providing you with high-quality services and attach great importance to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our services, as well as your rights.

Please read carefully and ensure that you fully understand the content of this Privacy Policy before using the App.

1. How We Collect and Use Your Information

The App primarily fetches public information via the Hacker News API and displays it on your device. We collect minimal necessary information following the principles of legitimacy, lawfulness, and necessity:

  • Network Communication: The App requires network access to connect to Hacker News servers to retrieve the latest stories, comments, and user details. Network request logs (such as IP addresses) may be recorded by the API provider (Firebase/Hacker News), but this is not collected directly by us.
  • Local Storage: The App may store your browsing history, favorites (bookmarks), and other data locally on your device to provide a better user experience. This data is only stored on your device and its associated iCloud account; we do not upload it to any other servers.
  • Web Browsing: When you click on a news link, the App loads third-party webpages via the built-in browser (WebView). Third-party websites may use Cookies or collect your visit information; please refer to the privacy policies of those third-party websites.
  • AI features (hosted mode, default): When you use title translation, summaries, article translation or the Agent, the story id, an article excerpt extracted on your device and the questions you type are sent to the HNEveryDay server (running on Cloudflare), which forwards them to the model provider (DeepSeek). Title translations and summaries are cached on the server as shared content; article translations and Agent conversations are not stored on the server.
  • Anonymous device identifier: To prevent abuse and count daily allowances, the App creates an anonymous device identifier with Apple App Attest. It is not linked to your identity. We do not collect names, email addresses, Apple IDs or advertising identifiers.
  • Subscriptions: When you buy Pro, the signed transaction provided by Apple (transaction id, product, expiry) is sent to our server to confirm your access. We never receive your payment details.
  • Own API key (advanced mode): In this mode summaries, translations and Agent conversations go directly from your device to the provider you configured and do not pass through HNEveryDay servers; your API key stays in the device Keychain. The one exception is the Agent's suggested questions: the App sends only the public story id and language to HNEveryDay servers to fetch questions shared by all users.

2. Data Storage

Storage Location: Data generated by the App (such as favorites, settings) is primarily stored locally on your device.

Storage Period: Local data will remains unless you actively delete it or uninstall the App.

3. Sharing, Transfer, and Public Disclosure

We do not sell or publicly disclose your personal information. The App has no account registration, so we do not hold your personal identity information. To provide AI features, the data described in section 1 is processed by: Cloudflare (server hosting), DeepSeek (model inference) and Apple (purchases and device attestation). Server logs and usage counters are kept for at most 90 days.

Cloudflare servers may be located outside mainland China; DeepSeek's model service is located in mainland China. Only the article excerpts and questions described above are sent, never information that identifies you. AI-generated content is labelled "AI-generated" in the App and keeps that label when copied or shared; use the "Report" button to flag inappropriate content and we will review and remove it.

4. Permissions

The permissions requested by the App are only used to provide core functions:

  • Network (Internet): Used to fetch news data and load webpages.

The App does not request or use sensitive permissions such as Camera, Photos, Location, Calendar, or Microphone.

5. Protection of Minors

We attach great importance to the protection of minors' personal information. If you are a minor under the age of 18, you should read and agree to this Privacy Policy under the supervision and guidance of your parents or other guardians before using this App.
